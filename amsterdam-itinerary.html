    Good to know

    • Tap in and out with a contactless bank card on trams, metro and trains. A GVB multi-day pass can work out cheaper if you use trams a lot.
    • Sunset is around 5:15pm at this time of year, so plan photos for late afternoon and blue hour just after.
    • Bring a waterproof layer; late October is often wet and windy.
    • Always check before ordering bitterballen or kroketten, as some are made with pork. De Pijp has many halal and pork-free options.
    • Walk on pavements, never in the red cycle lanes, and look both ways for bikes.
